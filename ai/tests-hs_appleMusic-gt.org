* The tests of 8pf.6.4

=tests/playback.test.js= tests playback, the track playing and the volume on whatever Music has
loaded, opening no album page. Before the run the user's Music was playing /Kind of Blue/, started
by opening its page, waiting 3 s, and sending =play= from stopped.

1. Music's volume is changed only while Music is paused, so nothing plays loudly, and the output
   device's volume is only set to the value it already has.
2. =currentTrackShow()= copies to the clipboard, so its test pauses the user's hs_clipboard-gt and
   puts the clipboard back, as hs_clipboard-gt's own tests do.
3. Music reports a track number of 0 for a track streamed from the catalogue, and "track 1 of
   current playlist" cannot be asked for one. Tracks are therefore told apart by name.
4. =hs.application.frontmostApplication()= does not exist in Hammerspoon 2; it is
   =hs.application.frontmost()=.

3. =trackPrevious()= may have failed only because Music answered slowly.

Bead 8pf.6.8 is to measure one question to Music, and how long =focus()= blocks Hammerspoon 2,

* The user's agreements

1. The tests may control the user's Music application: play, pause, skip, change the volume,
   and open album pages. The user said so on 2026-11-23.
2. The tests must not read or write the user's =albums.txt=. =tests/spoon.js= points the copy's
   =albumListPath= into the run's directory.
3. After a test, Music is paused again if it was not playing, and its volume is put back.
   The track it was on cannot be put back.

* How the Spoon plays an album from the catalogue

The Spoon's =play()=, and Play/Pause in the music menu, send AppleScript =play= or =playpause=.
They resume whatever Music already has queued.

Choose album, Random album and Auto-play play an album from the list, which is usually not in
the user's library. Music's AppleScript cannot play a catalogue album, so the Spoon opens the
album's page in Music and presses the page's own Play button through Accessibility
(=playButtonPressWhenReady()=).

These were tried by hand on 2026-11-23, to play an album without the button:

| Music before            | After opening the page                          | Result                     |
|-------------------------+-------------------------------------------------+----------------------------|
| Stopped, nothing loaded | Kind of Blue; =play= after 6 s                  | Played Kind of Blue        |
| Playing Kind of Blue    | In Rainbows; =play= after 3 s                   | Kept playing Kind of Blue  |
| Playing Kind of Blue    | In Rainbows; =stop=, then =play=, after 3 s     | Stopped, nothing loaded    |
| Stopped, nothing loaded | In Rainbows still showing; =play=               | Stopped, nothing loaded    |

So opening a page does not make its album the one Music plays, and =play= is not a replacement
for the button. =playpause= would pause a playing Music instead of switching albums.

* The freeze in the Play-button search

The Spoon found the button with =hs.ax.findByRole("AXButton", appElement)=, which walks Music's
whole Accessibility tree in one synchronous call. A probe on Kind of Blue's page measured each
call at 12.4 s and 11.7 s. Hammerspoon 2 is blocked for all of that time, and the button was
found after 24.6 s. The user saw a freeze of about 20 s. This happens in daily use, every time
Choose album, Random album or Auto-play plays an album, not only in the tests.

=hs.ax= offers no asynchronous search, and no search that can be limited in depth or count.

A probe of Kind of Blue's page, after 8 s of loading, counted 1306 elements: 456 static texts,
286 buttons, 254 cells, 229 rows, 34 groups, 12 images, 8 scroll areas, and a few others. The
Play button was at =AXWindow > AXSplitGroup > AXScrollArea > AXButton=, the fourth level from the
window.

The uncommitted change walks Music's windows itself, breadth first, without the menu bar:

1. no deeper than four levels below the window (=PLAY_SEARCH_DEPTH=);
2. not into =AXTable=, =AXOutline= or =AXRow= (=PLAY_SEARCH_SKIP=);
3. for at most 20 ms per timer tick (=PLAY_SEARCH_SLICE_MS=), carrying on at the next tick.

Measured results:

| Search                        | Result                                                                          |
|-------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| =findByRole=, whole tree      | 12.4 s and 11.7 s blocked per call; found after 24.6 s                          |
| Whole tree, in 20 ms slices   | Longest block 490 ms (one call); found after 21 s, 1176 elements, past the Spoon's 10 s limit |
| Top four levels, in slices    | Full run: Kind of Blue played in 2.1 s, longest block 0.22 s, every album found |
| Top four levels, in slices    | Album-skipping test: Kid A did not play within 20 s, no alert recorded          |
| Top four levels, in slices    | Album-skipping test: "Music did not offer a Play button for Miles Davis — Kind of Blue" |

The cause of the last two failures is not known. Three explanations have not been checked:

1. Music took longer than =playButtonTimeout=, 10 s, to render the page.
2. The button was deeper than four levels in that case.
3. Music was showing another view, such as Now Playing, rather than the album page.

The next step was a probe that opens the page and records, every second for 20 s, whether Play
is present and at which level, once from stopped and once while another album plays.

* Asking Music from the tests

=hs.osascript.applescriptSync()= blocks all of Hammerspoon 2 until Music answers. Music can take
minutes while it opens an album page: two questions once took more than two minutes, one of
them failing. The tests therefore ask with the asynchronous =hs.osascript.applescript()=, with a
10 s limit per question (=musicAsk()= in =tests/music.js=).

The Spoon itself still asks Music synchronously, in =musicTell()=. That has not been measured in
daily use.

Music answers slowly while it loads a page. A wait for a player state of 5 s was too short.

* Skipping between albums

Next album and Prev album skip one track at a time until the album changes. A test needs Music
to have another album queued before or after the one playing.

1. An album played from its page is followed by nothing. Next album reported "No other album
   within 20 tracks".
2. =play library playlist 1= with shuffle off took more than 15 s to start, then started in the
   middle of the following test, and broke two other tests.
3. Playing one album and then another, to leave the first in Music's history: not yet known.
   The run was stopped before the test.
4. The user's suggestion, =albumFinish()= in =tests/music.js=: play a few seconds of each track,
   move to the last, jump to one second before its end, and let it finish, so that the album is
   complete in the history. Not yet verified, because the album never started (the Play-button
   failure above).
5. The plan when the bead was suspended: a playlist, =testHS2=, which the user created and which
   is empty. AppleScript can copy only library tracks into a playlist, and Kind of Blue has no
   tracks in the library, so catalogue albums have to be added with Music's =⋯ → Add to
   Playlist=. Three short albums from the user's list were suggested: Miles Davis's /In a Silent
   Way/ (2 tracks), Brian Eno's /Ambient 1: Music for Airports/ (4) and /Kind of Blue/ (5 or 6).
   A test would then play it with =play playlist "testHS2"=, which needs no search, and could
   check both directions of skipping, landing on track 1. The playlist would also serve the
   tests of the track playing and the volume (8pf.6.4). It does not help the tests of Choose
   album, Random album and Auto-play, which need the Play button.

* Running the tests

1. A run of =play.test.js= takes about four minutes.
2. Tests already running inside Hammerspoon 2 cannot be stopped from outside. Stopping
   =tests/run= does not stop them; only a reload of the configuration does, and the harness then
   does not restore the focus. A reload also drops the password-like entries hs_clipboard-gt
   holds only in memory.
3. The harness reports a message only for a failing test. A measurement needed from a passing
   test was kept in a global variable for one run and read afterwards.
4. A test that waits for a Spoon to play should record alerts from its start
   (=t.alertsCapture()= in =needMusic()=), since the Spoon reports its failures only in alerts.
5. Results of the last complete run of =play.test.js=, with the four-level search: 8 passed, 1
   skipped (album skipping, nothing queued). The cases passing were playing by link, by name
   (Kind of Blue, with the alert for several matching releases), at random, an album the
   catalogue does not have, auto-play turning on, leaving a paused Music paused, and turning
   itself off with an empty list.
